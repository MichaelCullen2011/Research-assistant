## Source notes

### S01: Quantum in Defence & Government (draft for team discussion)
- **URL / file:** `inbox/Quantum_in_Defence__Government.html`
- **Author / publisher:** User's team; compiled from six research streams and a verification pass
- **Published:** 2026-09-30 (evidence to that date) · **Accessed:** 2026-10-10
- **Type / tier:** internal / internal
- **Access:** full-text
- **Why it matters:** Prior market and news scan that sets the D&G priorities this project builds on (PQC, sensing and PNT, quantum-inspired computing).
- **Key points:**
  - Window Oct 2021 – Sep 2026, UK-led with allied context; 90+ stories logged (header).
  - Maturity framing for computing: Quantum Inspired (classical methods drawn from quantum ideas, usable now), Quantum Utility, Quantum Advantage (Maturity map).
  - Cryptography is described as the only quantum workstream with a hard external clock; NCSC milestones 2028, 2031, 2035 (The view in seven points, point 2).
  - Sensing is described as closest to the field "but only for clocks and magnetic navigation"; five Royal Navy quantum PNT trials June 2025 – June 2026 (point 3).
  - Near-term optimisation value is described as quantum-inspired; cites a July 2026 Siemens study where a quantum-inspired annealer competed at industrial scale (point 4).
  - NCSC does not support QKD for government or military applications (point 7; Networks & QKD).
  - Microwave quantum radar rated "unsupported on the physics" (Maturity map, Sensing row).
  - Open question: how to adapt the Inspired / Utility / Advantage framing for sensing (Open questions).
- **Verbatim quotes:**
  > "Sensing is closest to the field, but only for clocks and magnetic navigation, and the UK route from trial to programme of record is not visible." (The view in seven points, point 3)
  > "Defence computing is a mid-term materials and chemistry story. Near-term optimisation value is quantum-inspired." (point 4)
- **Caveats:** Internal draft; it links to primary sources, but this project cites it only for the team's prior framing and priorities, and goes to primary sources for facts.
