# Search and selection record

Search date: 12 October 2026. Inclusive publication window: 1 January 2016–12 October 2026. Four priority strands; UK/European relevance first, global science eligible.

Recorded 96 screening records across 82 candidate identity groups after title/DOI/arXiv de-duplication. Counts include title-only leads, not just opened abstracts. There are 24 core full-paper reviews and 2 contextual opened sources; duplicate versions count as one study.

## Method and limitations

- Luna supplied bounded bibliographic leads; Sol selected and reviewed primary texts. Search snippets supplied discovery leads only, never performance evidence.
- Selection considered relevance, distinct contributions, experimental realism, access, strong comparators and negative results. Searches included critiques and forward version/correction checks.
- An explicit 2016–2020 follow-up includes early/summer2016 leads. Full reviews concentrate on 2021–2026 journal publications and preprints back to2020. Older accessible-paper coverage is thinner; this is not a comprehensive decade review.
- The Hosten2016 paper and its Fermilab PDF lead were attempted by the orchestrator: publisher access failed and the mirror required login. Neither was logged as an opened source.
- Article-level forward-reference searching was bounded, not exhaustive database citation harvesting. No claim of independent replication absence outside this search.
- Six sensing and five optimisation/emerging studies received independent Astra challenge records. The second batch saved complete records before its turn ended with a usage-limit error; the orchestrator checked the recorded conclusions. Optional Greve challenge adjudication was not completed.
- No paper code/data was executed; no paid access used.
