# Research brief: Critical academic scan of quantum priorities

## Question
Which academic results published from 1 January 2016 to 12 October 2026 offer credible advances relevant to emerging defence and government priorities, and which conclusions fail scrutiny or exceed the evidence?

### Sub-questions
1. What do navigation and timing papers demonstrate, particularly under realistic motion, noise and GNSS denial?
2. What do detection and situational-awareness papers demonstrate, including gravity, magnetic, RF, radar and distributed sensing?
3. Which quantum-inspired optimisation methods survive comparison with competitive classical methods on relevant workloads?
4. Which emerging quantum phenomena offer substantiated improvements, and where do scaling or engineering assumptions break the application chain?
5. Which findings withstand independent challenge, and what evidence would change the assessment?

## Purpose & audience
Evidence for Michael Cullen's quantum defence and government research and subsequent whitepaper. Explain scientific contributions, critical limitations and application relevance to mixed technical readers. This task is an academic scan, separate from the preceding programme and phenomena survey.

## Scope
- **Time window:** 1 January 2016 to 12 October 2026 inclusive; prioritise 2021 onward without excluding valuable earlier work. Record first preprint and journal dates separately, plus version examined. Older foundations can be context only, flagged outside the window.
- **Region:** UK and Europe first for application relevance; include global physics and methods research without geographical exclusion.
- **In scope:** peer-reviewed journal/conference papers, preprints, supplements, corrections, replication and rebuttal papers; full-text methodological review; navigation/timing, detection/awareness, quantum-inspired optimisation and emerging phenomena.
- **Lower priority:** quantum computing and cryptographic risk, only where directly material to these strands. No general PQC/computing survey.
- **Out of scope:** market sizing, vendor procurement recommendations, paid subscriptions, exhaustive systematic-review claims and experimental replication.
