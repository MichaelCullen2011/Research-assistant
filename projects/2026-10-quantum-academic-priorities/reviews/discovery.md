# Bibliographic discovery handoff

Screened 28 distinct candidates across the two requested strands. The JSON records exact metadata where verified, access notes, whether a primary landing page was opened, a short selection reason, a priority, and the main question for deeper review. Journal/preprint duplicates were treated as one study. Search-result-only candidates remain marked `opened: false`; they are discovery leads, not logged research sources. No performance numbers have been inferred from search snippets.

## Recommended first review set

### Quantum-inspired methods

1. [High-performance combinatorial optimization based on classical mechanics](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abe7953) — established simulated-bifurcation anchor; inspect benchmark and baseline quality.
2. [Benchmark of quantum-inspired heuristic solvers for QUBO](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-06070-5) — comparative results vary by problem family; useful against one-winner narratives.
3. [Transport robot scheduling case study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.09736) — application-led digital annealer, hybrid annealer and Gurobi comparison.
4. [Closing the Quantum-Classical Scaling Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22514) — direct rebuttal to a quantum-annealing scaling claim; check runtime accounting and extrapolation.
5. [Limitations of tensor-network approaches](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.23.054049) — independent challenge with geometry-dependent tensor-network performance.
6. [A Reality Check on Quantum Optimisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13325) — recent industrial scheduling study with classical and hardware comparisons; preprint status matters.

### Emerging quantum phenomena

1. [Field Demonstration of Distributed Quantum Sensing without Post-Selection](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.031009) — established distributed-sensing field anchor; test practical network overheads.
2. [Distributed sensing with a mode-entangled spin-squeezed atomic network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06382) — connects nonlocal entanglement with clock and interferometer use cases; inspect demonstrated versus protocol results.
3. [Distributed sensing of multiple phases with fewer photons](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-44204-z) — experimental multi-node/fiber result; check resource counting and field relevance.
4. [Antenna-based and Rydberg RF sensor comparison](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0222827) — direct conventional-sensor comparison and an important counterweight to broad sensitivity claims.
5. [Radar Ranging Using a Rydberg Atomic Homodyne Receiver](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11833) — recent experimental advance with clear relevance, requiring matched radar baselines.
6. [Superextensive electrical power from a quantum battery](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-026-02240-6), read alongside [Hamiltonian locality and quantum-battery charging limits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12000) — pair an emerging experimental cycle claim with resource-bound theory; practical energy relevance remains remote.

## Coverage gaps and cautions

- This is targeted candidate discovery, not the brief's 60–100-candidate screen or a systematic review. Search coverage was strongest for simulated bifurcation, tensor networks, distributed sensing, Rydberg RF and batteries.
- Navigation and timing have plausible leads through atomic networks, Earth-field magnetometry and atom interferometry, but few candidates directly test motion, GNSS denial, field drift or end-to-end inertial performance. A deeper reviewer should prioritize those missing operational conditions.
- Quantum-inspired logistics evidence is uneven: some results are preprints, application models can simplify constraints, and solver timing may omit formulation and preprocessing costs. The reviewer should favor matched, independently reproducible classical baselines.
- Quantum battery and topological/critical-sensing papers are retained as lower-priority phenomena where the application chain is speculative. Topological sensitivity can arise classically; response amplification alone does not establish a quantum sensing gain.
